Renovation wiring in North York.

A North York kitchen renovation often brings a new appliance list, and each item on it can change the circuits the room needs. Settle the list early so the wiring plan follows it.

Electrical decisions belong early in the renovation

A renovation changes how the space will be used and what equipment it must serve. Provide the drawings and appliance specifications before the electrical scope is finalized. A Licensed Electrical Contractor can assess retained wiring, new circuits and the proposed loads. Municipal building permissions and ESA electrical notification are separate matters, and the project may require both.

Prepare the scope for your North York property

Share the kitchen drawings and the specification sheets for every appliance, including an induction cooktop, wall oven or dishwasher. Mark any wall shared with the neighbour where outlets or cables are planned.

Ask which appliances need dedicated circuits, whether the new list fits within the existing supply and when the electrical work will be reviewed. Agree who approves changes if the appliance list changes during construction.

What should the assessment establish?

Starting from the final appliance list rather than the old layout, the contractor's scope records which circuits are new, which existing wiring stays and how the ESA notification fits the construction schedule. An induction cooktop, a separate wall oven and a larger refrigerator can each change the circuit plan, and together they may affect the supply decision for the whole home. The list itself is yours: if other upgrades such as a charger are coming, add them now. Close the walls of the North York kitchen only after the electrical rough-in has been reviewed.

Ontario electrical safety

ESA says almost all electrical work needs a notification of work. If you hire someone for home electrical work, use a Licensed Electrical Contractor. Agree who files the notification and request the Certificate of Acceptance after ESA review.

Read the current ESA notification and inspection guidance, contractor hiring guidance. You can verify an electrical contractor with ESA before agreeing to work.

Questions before you agree to work

Should I choose kitchen appliances before the electrical plan is set?

Choosing them first gives the contractor real specifications to work from. If a choice is still open, name the options so the circuits can suit the likely model, and flag any later change before the walls are closed.

Does a kitchen renovation need an ESA notification as well as a permit?

A building permit and an ESA notification of work are separate. Ask the contractor to confirm which apply to your project, who files each one and when the electrical work will be reviewed during construction.

  • Construction schedule and other trades

Only photograph labels and locations that you can see safely. Do not remove electrical covers or approach damaged equipment to gather information.
